# Move for Skills — coach card

One page for a teacher or outside coach taking a sport group. No set-up, no Sheet, no grading. Four moments in the app, everything else on paper.

**Before you start**
- You need to sign in with the school Google account you were given. Open the link on a laptop or your phone. If it says *Teachers only*, tell the PE teacher who runs the Sheet.
- Read the **unit plan** page you were handed (tests, drill steps, personal skills). Cross out and rewrite anything you would coach differently and hand it back. The Sheet gets updated for you.
- You get a paper log for every student. Students keep it for the whole unit. It is three things: the three tests, a line for their chosen skill, and the drill steps.

**What the app shows you**
- The blue line at the top says what today needs from you, if anything, and an *Open* button takes you there.
- Check-ins: one page per check-in, one row per student. Students: each student's page. Print: the paper logs and the unit plan.

| Lesson | Students | You |
|---|---|---|
| **1–2** | Intro. Three skill tests in pairs, same set-up every time, partner counts aloud, scores on the paper log. No laptops. | Mark the feed spots and zones with cones before the lesson. Hand out the paper logs. Watch. Spot-check a few counts. |
| **3** · laptops first, ~8 min | Type the three scores from the paper, pick their focus skill, tidy the drafted goal, answer one question. | **Check-ins → Early**, same day, ~3 min: glance at each row, fix a wrong score, tap ✓, one tap for personal skills (Not yet / Sometimes / Consistently). |
| **3–5** | Then work the drill steps for their focus skill. A partner initials when the *done when* line is met. | Circulate. **Sign the step** on the paper when it is met so they move on. Nothing in the app. |
| **6** · laptops | Retest the focus skill, type the score, tap the step reached, one reflection. Finished all steps? They choose an extension skill. | **Check-ins → Middle**: check the score, ✓, one personal-skills tap. |
| **7–8** | Drills. | Sign paper. Nothing in the app. |
| **9–10** | Game play. | **Check-ins → Game play**, on your phone while you watch: one row per student with their focus skill. Tap the level you see (7 · 6–5 · 4–3 · 2–1). A note is optional. This is the skill grade. |
| **10** · laptops | Tap the step reached, place themselves, self-rate personal skills, two reflections. | **Check-ins → End**: type each student's final retest score, ✓, one personal-skills tap. |

**The levels, in a game**
- **7** the skill is automatic
- **6–5** usually works, some errors
- **4–3** works in easy situations
- **2–1** rarely works yet

**Personal skills** (one tap, for the stretch since the last check-in): self-management, perseverance, collaboration. What you saw is the evidence. Not yet / Sometimes / Consistently.

**If something goes wrong**
- A yellow bar saying *sending in the background* is fine. Do not reload; it clears itself.
- Wrong score? Type over it. Wrong tap? Tap it again to undo.
- Grades are done by the PE teacher from what you tapped. You do not need the Grades page.
